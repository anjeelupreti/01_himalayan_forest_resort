Room Overview

Indulge in the ultimate luxury experience with our Super Deluxe Room at Himalaya Forest Resort. This spacious retreat features floor-to-ceiling windows showcasing panoramic views of Begnas Lake and the surrounding Himalayan mountains, creating a serene atmosphere perfect for romantic getaways or peaceful vacations.

The elegantly appointed room seamlessly blends modern comfort with traditional Nepali design elements. The space includes a comfortable seating area with cozy chairs, a work desk for business travelers, and a private balcony where you can enjoy your morning tea while watching the sunrise over the lake. The bedroom features a plush king-size bed or twin beds with luxury linens, ensuring a restful night's sleep.

Each Super Deluxe Room is thoughtfully designed to provide maximum comfort and convenience, with attention to every detail from the locally-inspired artwork to the premium amenities that make your stay truly memorable.
